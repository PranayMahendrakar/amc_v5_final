How the extraction works

  1. 01

    Locate

    Two-layer detection. Canonical headline match (e.g. “Balance Sheet as at March 31, 2025”) for the common case; structural fingerprint (Particulars + dual As at headers + Equity/Assets anchors) for headerless pages that ship with no title.

  2. 02

    Extract

    Span-level PyMuPDF geometry. Numeric tokens form right-edge clusters that define columns. Rows are Y-banded. A 25%-of-page-width gap triggers side-by-side spread mode, splitting at the physical page midpoint. Unlabeled Schedule III subtotals are preserved, not eaten.

  3. 03

    Classify

    A YAML alias dictionary maps every label to one of the spec's 26 buckets. Section context disambiguates polysemous labels (current vs non-current Borrowings). RapidFuzz token-set fallback catches typos. No model in the loop — every classification is auditable.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Sum into the template, compute Total Assets and Total Liabilities + Equity, and flag any gap above the ₹1 tolerance. Outputs a trimmed source PDF you can open side-by-side with the computed template to verify every number visually.
